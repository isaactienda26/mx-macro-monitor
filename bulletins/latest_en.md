### The peso weakens 4.87% on the week, its largest weekly move since June 2024

- FIX at 19.3668 MXN per USD (Nov 24, 2026): +4.87% w/w, +3.75% m/m, +4.69% y/y. A higher FIX means a weaker peso.
- Statistical signal: atypical (+3.39 standard deviations vs. weekly moves over the past 5 years); the largest weekly move in that direction since the week of Jun 7, 2024.
- 52-week range: 16.87 (Sep 4, 2026) to 19.37 (Nov 24, 2026); the current level is the period high.

> Why it matters: the exchange rate passes through to imported-goods prices and to costs of firms with dollar inputs.

### Economic activity contracts 0.7% m/m in September

- Seasonally adjusted IGAE: −0.7% m/m and +3.0% y/y in Sep-2026 (prior month: +0.4% and +3.7%).
- Last 3 months vs. previous 3: +0.9%. This smooths single-month noise.
- Statistical signal: unusual monthly change (−1.58 standard deviations vs. the previous 60 months).

> Why it matters: IGAE is the monthly proxy for GDP and anticipates the quarterly print.

### Inflation rises to 3.75% in October, 3 months up in a row

- Headline: 3.75% YoY in Oct-2026 (+0.30 pp m/m), inside Banxico's 2%–4% band, 0.75 pp above the 3% target.
- Core: 3.80% (+0.05 pp). Headline and core are running at similar levels.
- 12-month expectation (median, Banxico survey, Oct-2026): 4.04%, vs. 4.16% the previous month.

> Why it matters: core inflation is the gauge Banxico watches to set rates; its distance from target defines the room for further cuts.

### Banxico rate at 6.25%; spread over the Fed at 225 bp

- Last move: −25 bp, effective Nov 6, 2026; −100 bp over 12 months.
- Ex-ante real rate: 2.23% (policy rate minus 12-month inflation expectation); ex-post: 2.50%.
- Fed: 3.75%–4.00% range. Banxico–Fed spread (upper bound): 225 bp vs. 325 bp a year ago.

> Why it matters: a narrower spread lowers the relative yield on peso assets, one of the supports of the exchange rate.
